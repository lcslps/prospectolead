# Niche Visual Grammar: Veterinary and Pet

Use for veterinary care, pet shops and animal services. Choose the applicable tone:
calm care for veterinary, more vibrant product energy for retail pet.

- Prioritize emotionally credible animal-and-care imagery, natural surfaces and clear
  routes to real contact/location information.
- Veterinary design should feel reassuring and professional, never childish. Pet
  retail may use brighter color and playful forms, but must retain readable hierarchy.
- Never claim procedures, emergency care, professionals, results, products, stock or
  service availability without verification.
- On mobile, keep animal faces and human interaction clear and avoid crops that remove
  the relationship the image is meant to convey.
