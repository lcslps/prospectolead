# Niche Visual Grammar: Food and Hospitality Campaign

Use for restaurants, burger shops, pizzerias, sushi, acai, cafes and hospitality.
The visual language can be campaign-like, but the business facts must remain exact.

- Put a real dish, ambience or product image at the center when available. Select one
  sensory focal point instead of many unrelated stock images.
- Burger and fast food can use bold display type, close product crops and graphic
  energy. Pizza can be editorial, street or warm Italian. Sushi benefits from precise,
  dramatic composition. Acai can be energetic and fruit-led. Choose only one coherent
  direction for the actual business.
- Keep menu, delivery, reservation, opening-hour and price claims out unless verified.
- Make the real contact, map, reservation or WhatsApp action easy to find.
- Avoid generic restaurant cards, fake menu items, fake customer quotes and stock that
  is presented as the establishment.
