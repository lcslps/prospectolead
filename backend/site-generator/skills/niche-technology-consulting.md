# Niche Visual Grammar: Technology and Consulting

Use for SaaS, consulting, professional technology and advanced services.

- Favor a restrained dark or high-clarity direction, strong headline, deliberate
  information hierarchy and a small number of evidence surfaces.
- Use data, diagrams, product fragments and technical motifs only when they represent
  supplied facts or are clearly illustrative. Avoid generic gradients, glowing cards
  and a fake dashboard as decoration.
- The CTA should lead to a verified contact path. Do not create customer logos,
  outcomes, integrations, metrics, certificates or case studies without evidence.
- Mobile should simplify the composition while preserving the primary proof and action.
