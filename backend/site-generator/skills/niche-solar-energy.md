# Niche Visual Grammar: Solar Energy

Use for solar installation, clean energy and adjacent verified energy businesses.

- Convey technology, investment and sustainability through contemporary architecture,
  credible solar imagery, measured data and a clear consultation/contact action.
- Favor an image-led hero of panels, installation context or clean architecture; use
  sky, green and warm energy accents with restraint rather than an all-purpose
  environmental gradient.
- Floating benefit or proof modules are useful only with verified facts. Do not invent
  savings, guarantees, monitoring, capacity, installation counts or certifications.
- Use technical clarity and a robust mobile layout; ensure light text is never placed
  over an uncontrolled bright sky or panel crop.
