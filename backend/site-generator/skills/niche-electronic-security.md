# Niche Visual Grammar: Electronic Security

Use for electronic security, monitoring, cameras, access control and related services.

- Build a composed dark technical direction: deep neutral surfaces, a restrained cyan
  or signal accent, short confident headline and relevant professional imagery.
- Use camera, control-room or installation imagery only as illustration unless it is a
  supplied real business asset. Avoid decorative HUDs, fake dashboards and unsupported
  claims about 24-hour monitoring, coverage or response time.
- Integrate factual proof and the contact action into the scene with clear contrast.
- Keep the technical treatment legible and calm on mobile instead of shrinking a dense
  desktop dashboard.
