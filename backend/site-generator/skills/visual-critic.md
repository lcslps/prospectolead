# Visual Critic

## Internal review before delivery

Review the generated page against the chosen visual contract, not against a vague
idea of "premium". Inspect the hero, a middle section, the final CTA/footer, overlays
and the primary action at 1440px, 768px and 390px when the environment supports it.

Check:

- the page has a dominant, segment-appropriate hero rather than a generic template;
- type hierarchy, line breaks, spacing and media crops still support the message;
- real assets are used where available and no image is empty, broken or misleading;
- buttons, links, local information and contact actions are clear and factually valid;
- cards, gradients, radius, shadows and motion are intentional rather than repeated;
- mobile reflows cleanly with no clipping, overlap or hidden primary action;
- focus, contrast and reduced motion remain usable.

When a problem is found, make the smallest coherent repair that preserves verified
facts and the chosen direction. Do not claim a screenshot, accessibility scan or
responsive test was performed unless it actually ran.
