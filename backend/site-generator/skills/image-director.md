# Image Director

## Define image direction before requesting an asset

For every important image, state its role and an executable direction:

- subject and factual status (real business photo or illustrative stock);
- placement and aspect ratio;
- camera distance or lens feel, angle and focal subject;
- lighting, material, depth and mood;
- composition and negative space reserved for nearby HTML text;
- desktop focal point and mobile crop strategy;
- what must be avoided, including text, logos, watermarks, distorted architecture,
  irrelevant people and generic staged stock.

Example form: `wide architectural photograph of a stone kitchen, countertop on the
right, soft side light, quiet left negative space for HTML headline, 16:9 hero,
preserve countertop detail on mobile; no text or logos`.

## Asset truth and treatment

- Use real business assets first where they establish trust. Preserve their identity,
  details and attribution.
- Stock is illustrative only. Never label it as the business, team, work, facility or
  customer.
- Use supplied asset tokens or declared image intents only; never invent URLs.
- Give meaningful alt text, intentional object-position and lazy loading below the
  fold. Important visual media needs a coherent fallback, never an empty block.
- Do not put headlines, menus, logos, UI or factual claims inside generated imagery;
  build those in HTML and CSS.
